Room by room

Our requirements what we need · In the 3D model how it is shown today (open to your ideas)

Whole home

approx. 44′ × 39′

Our requirements

  • Warm, elegant modern Indian interiors (cream, light oak, beige marble, brass, olive, terracotta)
  • Door and window positions exactly as per the builder plan
  • False ceilings with warm cove lighting; ceiling fans in living areas and bedrooms
  • Plenty of closed storage, with lofts up to the ceiling where possible

In the 3D model

  • Beige marble in common areas, wood flooring in bedrooms
  • Cove lights, downlights and fans shown in every main room

Drawing room

13′7″ × 12′0″

Our requirements

  • Traditional jhula (swing) in the drawing room, open — no partition or separation
  • L-shaped sofa
  • TV wall
  • Shoe rack outside the house, not inside
  • Brass urli and lamps, a near wall-to-wall rug, indoor plants
  • Look and colours like the warm beige reference: cream, oak, olive

In the 3D model

  • Carved teak jhula on brass chains near the master-bedroom door
  • L-sofa in the south-west corner facing the jhula and TV
  • TV wall: limewash panel in an oak frame, floating shelves, fluted console
  • Shoe rack with mirror in the entrance lobby

Living

12′0″ × 8′8″

Our requirements

  • Only one sofa
  • No TV unit — the entrance wall should be a design feature instead
  • The feature should run along the entire wall, up to the washroom (C.TOI) door
  • No god idol on this wall
  • Projector for movies

In the 3D model

  • 7′ three-seater facing the wall
  • Fluted oak + backlit onyx band + brass inlays + floating ledge; Bedroom 2 door hidden flush
  • Ceiling projector behind the sofa with a recessed roll-down screen

Dining

16′2″ × 12′0″

Our requirements

  • Crockery unit on the dining wall

In the 3D model

  • 6-seater oval table and ring chandelier
  • Full-height crockery unit: fluted base, quartz top, backlit glass display, loft

Kitchen

13′11″ × 8′0″

Our requirements

  • Pantry unit beside the fridge
  • Appliance shutter (to hide mixer, toaster, kettle)
  • Sink towards the end, near the utility door
  • Dishwasher placement
  • Wicker baskets
  • Lofts on both sides
  • Keep the window to the utility

In the 3D model

  • U-shape: fridge + tall pull-out pantry, appliance garage with roll-up shutter on the north counter
  • Hob + chimney on the east wall
  • South run: sink by the utility door → dishwasher → wicker baskets
  • Sage base cabinets, white uppers with under-lights, lofts both sides, window kept open between cabinets

Puja room

4′8″ × 5′6″

Our requirements

  • God should face towards the main entrance
  • Small window as per plan
  • The window wall should also be highlighted

In the 3D model

  • Teak mandir on the east wall, deity facing west (the closest straight direction to the entrance)
  • Backlit white CNC jaali wall around the small high window, brass frame and diya ledge
  • Jaali doors, toran, bell, kolam rug

Master bedroom

17′2″ × 12′0″

Our requirements

  • Full-length wardrobe along one entire wall
  • Dressing unit on the TV-unit wall, next to the wardrobe
  • Work station in the room

In the 3D model

  • King bed on the south wall with a padded headboard wall
  • Wardrobe + loft along the full west wall
  • Dressing unit beside the wardrobe; TV console on the same wall
  • Work station beside the washroom (M.TOI) door

Bedroom 1

11′5″ × 12′0″

Our requirements

  • Bed size 6′ × 5′
  • Wardrobe on the window wall, continuing into a window seat and then a dressing unit — the window comes in the middle
  • No study table
  • No bedside table on the wardrobe side
  • Washroom (TOI-01) door as per plan

In the 3D model

  • Bed against a painted terracotta arch, one bedside table on the door side
  • One continuous oak unit: wardrobe → cushioned window seat → dressing with lit mirror, loft over all

Bedroom 2

12′0″ × 12′0″

Our requirements

  • Bed size 6′ × 4′, no bedside tables
  • Work station at the L-shaped corner window
  • Cupboard on the left of the door, book shelf on the right
  • Book shelf L-shaped, turning into the corner
  • Bed towards the toilet/balcony doors, but with enough space to use them

In the 3D model

  • Bed on the east wall with about 2½′ clear to the doors
  • L-shaped desk under the L window with roller blinds
  • Cupboard with loft left of the door; L-shaped open book shelf right of the door
