Room by room

Drawing room

13′7″ × 12′0″
  • L-shaped sofa in the south-west corner, under the window
  • Carved teak jhula on brass chains near the master-bedroom door, no partition
  • TV wall on the north wall: limewash panel, oak frame, floating shelves, fluted console
  • Shoe rack and mirror outside the main door
  • Large jute rug, ceiling fan, cove-lit false ceiling

Living

12′0″ × 8′8″
  • One 7′ three-seater sofa only, no TV
  • Entrance wall designed end to end (living through dining, up to the C.TOI door): fluted oak, backlit onyx band, brass inlays, floating ledge
  • Bedroom 2 door hidden flush in the panelling
  • Ceiling projector with a recessed roll-down screen

Dining

16′2″ × 12′0″
  • 6-seater oval table, ring chandelier
  • Full-height crockery unit on the dining wall: fluted base, quartz top, backlit glass display, loft

Kitchen

13′11″ × 8′0″
  • U-shaped modular kitchen, sage base cabinets, quartz top
  • Tall pull-out pantry beside the fridge
  • Appliance garage with a roll-up shutter (mixer, toaster, kettle)
  • Sink at the end near the utility door, dishwasher next to it, then wicker baskets
  • Hob + chimney on the east wall; keep the window to the utility open
  • Wall cabinets and lofts on both sides

Puja room

4′8″ × 5′6″
  • Mandir on the east wall so the deity faces west, toward the main-entrance side
  • Window wall highlighted with a backlit white CNC jaali around the small high window
  • Jaali doors, toran, bell, kolam rug

Master bedroom

17′2″ × 12′0″
  • King bed (6′ × 7′) on the south wall with a padded headboard wall
  • Full-length wardrobe with loft along the entire west wall
  • Dressing unit on the TV wall, right beside the wardrobe
  • Work station beside the washroom (M.TOI) door
  • TV console on the north wall

Bedroom 1

11′5″ × 12′0″
  • Bed 6′ × 5′, one bedside table on the door side only
  • West wall as one continuous unit: wardrobe → window seat (window in the middle) → dressing unit, loft over all
  • No study table; TOI-01 door in the north-east corner

Bedroom 2

12′0″ × 12′0″
  • Bed 6′ × 4′, no bedside tables, about 2½′ clear from the toilet and balcony doors
  • L-shaped work station under the L-shaped corner window
  • Beside the entry door: cupboard on the left, L-shaped book shelf on the right turning into the corner

Room sizes are from the builder's floor plan. Window and door positions in the 3D model were matched to the plan by eye — please confirm all dimensions on site before production drawings.
